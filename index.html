Run an experience-curve analysis

Data boundary: The ten FY2016–2025 financial observations come from Chipotle SEC Form 10-Ks. The default COGS proxy is the SUM of reported food/packaging, restaurant labor, occupancy, and other restaurant operating costs; this is not a standalone GAAP COGS line and excludes separately reported depreciation, G&A, and pre-opening expenses. The cost-definition control also permits a food-only sensitivity comparison. Actual companywide order counts and dollar average checks are unavailable. The default $15 check is an illustrative entrée-plus-side basket, NOT a reported average: a 2025 Chipotle spokesperson described a chicken entrée at roughly $10, while a representative official menu lists chips and guacamole at $4.90 (different snapshot/location). The default 3% annual increase is the nearest whole-percent approximation of reported 2022–2025 comparable-store average-check increases (7.1%, 2.9%, 2.1%, 1.2%; mean 3.325%). Extrapolating that recent average over earlier years is a modeling choice, not measured historical order pricing. Change assumptions to stress-test the result.

Bring your own 10-K data for any other ticker

This static site does not automatically fetch SEC filings. To avoid presenting unaudited or guessed data as verified, it accepts values extracted from the company's filings. Enter a ticker above and import its corresponding CSV (5+ distinct years). Headers: year,revenue_m,cogs_m,units_m,avg_price,filing_url (or food_revenue_m instead of revenue_m). Use units_m when disclosed; otherwise leave it empty and supply avg_price per year or use the assumption controls. All monetary amounts and direct units are in millions. A direct-unit dataset bypasses the revenue proxy.

Source data stays in your browser. The calculator does not fabricate figures when another ticker is entered.

Built-in dataset: Chipotle Mexican Grill (CMG), 2016-2025; SEC 10-Ks. Recalculate to explore different scenarios.
